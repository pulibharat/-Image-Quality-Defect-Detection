What this tool analyzes ▾

Every upload runs through the same pipeline: 12 engineered image statistics are computed, fed into 6 independent gradient-boosted classifiers (one per issue below), and combined into a 0–100 quality score. A separate lightweight autoencoder adds a reconstruction-error heatmap for localization/explainability. No external AI APIs are used — every model runs locally.

Detection categories
🌫️
Blur / sharpness

Laplacian variance & Tenengrad gradient magnitude — low values mean few sharp edges.

🌑
Underexposure

Share of near-black pixels and overall brightness mean.

☀️
Overexposure

Share of clipped near-white pixels and elevated brightness.

📶
Noise

Immerkær fast noise-variance estimate from a Laplacian-kernel convolution.

🧩
Corruption

Blockiness, entropy, and edge-density patterns typical of heavy compression or transmission damage.

🩹
Defect

Texture/edge irregularities consistent with scratches, spots, or sensor defects.

12 image statistics computed per upload

Sharpness

  • Laplacian variance
  • Tenengrad gradient magnitude

Exposure & contrast

  • Brightness mean
  • Contrast (std. dev.)
  • Underexposed pixel ratio
  • Overexposed pixel ratio

Noise & texture

  • Noise sigma
  • Entropy
  • Edge density
  • Blockiness

Color

  • Colorfulness
  • Saturation mean
How the quality score is calculated

Starting from 100, each detected issue subtracts weight × severity × confidence (corruption and defect are weighted heaviest since they indicate the most severe problems). ACCEPTABLE means no issues and a score ≥ 75, DEFECTIVE means a high-severity corruption/defect or a score below 40, and everything else is DEGRADED.
